Methodology & limitations

Meridian is an independent demonstration built to illustrate bank treasury and product-pricing decision processes. Historical Seacoast financials and peer data are labeled as reported observations; forecast assumptions are illustrative.

Three types of input

1. Publicly reported information

Balance-sheet amounts, aggregate interest measures, and AFS/HTM cost and fair-value categories are drawn from source-linked public disclosures through 2026 Q3.

2. Hypothetical scenario parameters

Deposit beta, asset repricing shares, rate paths, growth, spread shocks, duration by security category, convexity and principal recycling are stress-model assumptions, not verified actual sensitivities.

3. Synthetic behavior data

Customer attrition examples are simulated. Statistical holdout results validate only the example workflow, not predictive quality for actual bank customers.

What the outputs mean

GAAP net interest income sensitivity, funds-transfer-pricing contribution, and estimated investment portfolio price changes are different measures. They must not be summed without accounting and exposure reconciliation. Risk estimates are not an AOCI or regulatory capital forecast.

The public demonstration has no live data feed. Inputs must be refreshed and reconciled before presenting newer periods.

For complete equations and methods, review the full treasury model methodology and the pricing and behavior methodology.

What production use would require

Verified account-level cash flows; source controls and lineage; security-level positions, prepayment and spread analytics; independent model back-testing; secure authentication and permissions; and formal validation and management approval.
